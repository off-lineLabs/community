# Offline Labs Community

Welcome to the Offline Labs community! 🚀

## About Offline Labs

Offline Labs is a community-driven project dedicated to creating free, open source, and ad-free alternatives for everyday apps. We believe in empowering users with software that respects their privacy, works offline, and remains truly open.

Learn more at **[offline-labs.com](http://offline-labs.com/)**

## Core Principles

### 🔌 Offline First
Our applications are designed to work seamlessly without an internet connection. We believe that access to your tools and data shouldn't depend on connectivity.

### 🔒 Privacy by Design
Privacy isn't an afterthought—it's fundamental to everything we build. Your data stays yours, and we design our applications with privacy as a core requirement from day one.

### 🌐 Open Source
All our code is open source. We believe in transparency, community collaboration, and giving users full control over the software they use.

## Get Involved

We welcome contributions from everyone! Whether you're a developer, designer, writer, or just passionate about privacy and open source, there's a place for you here.

### Ways to Contribute

- **Code**: Submit pull requests to improve our projects
- **Documentation**: Help us write better docs
- **Design**: Create UI/UX improvements
- **Testing**: Report bugs and test new features
- **Community**: Help others, answer questions, share ideas

### Getting Started

1. Read our [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md)
2. Check out our [Contributing Guidelines](CONTRIBUTING.md)
3. Join the discussions to connect with the community
4. Pick an issue labeled `good first issue` to get started

## Community Guidelines

- **Follow coding style guidelines**: Maintain consistency across our projects
- **Write clear, documented code**: Make it easy for others to understand and build upon your work
- **Test your changes thoroughly**: Quality matters
- **Keep commits focused and atomic**: One logical change per commit
- **Be respectful and inclusive**: We're all here to learn and grow together
- **Help others learn and grow**: Share your knowledge and experience
- **Share knowledge and experience**: We all benefit from each other's insights
